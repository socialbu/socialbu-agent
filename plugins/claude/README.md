# SocialBu

Plan social media content, schedule posts, and review results from your connected SocialBu accounts in Claude.

The plugin includes two skills:

- **Social Media Scheduler:** work with connected accounts, drafts, scheduled posts, publishing queues, and analytics through SocialBu.
- **Social Media Content Calendar:** turn your brief or existing content into a dated calendar with drafts tailored to each network.

## Connect your account

Authorize the SocialBu connection in your browser when Claude prompts you. The plugin connects to `https://socialbu.com/mcp` using OAuth. Keep passwords and access tokens out of chat.

Planning a calendar works without an account. Saving drafts, scheduling posts, and accessing account data require a SocialBu account. See [SocialBu pricing](https://socialbu.com/pricing) for the trial and subscription options.

## Try it

- "Turn this campaign brief into a two-week content calendar."
- "Show my connected accounts and posts scheduled for next week."
- "Save these approved captions as drafts for my selected account."
- "Compare the performance of my posts from last month."

Requests to SocialBu send the relevant content and account identifiers to SocialBu's hosted service. The tools return data from the account you authorize. Available operations depend on your plan, permissions, and connected accounts. Publishing, scheduling, and account changes follow your instructions.
